About Jakarta

Jakarta, formerly known as Batavia, is the capital and largest city of Indonesia. Located on the northwest coast of Java island, it's a bustling metropolis that seamlessly blends modern skyscrapers with traditional Indonesian culture.

With a population exceeding 10 million people in the metropolitan area, Jakarta is one of Southeast Asia's most important economic centers. The city serves as Indonesia's political, financial, and cultural hub, attracting millions of visitors annually to explore its rich history, vibrant street life, and diverse attractions.

Top Attractions

Historical Sites

Museum Nasional

Indonesia's national museum featuring artifacts from ancient civilizations, traditional crafts, and cultural heritage spanning thousands of years.

Istana Merdeka

The Independence Palace where Indonesia declared independence in 1945. The building features beautiful Dutch colonial architecture.

Kota Tua

An ancient town dating back to the 17th century, offering a glimpse into Jakarta's colonial past with traditional houses and narrow streets.

Natural Attractions

Jakarta Bay

A beautiful coastal area perfect for sunset viewing, water sports, and enjoying the sea breeze while watching boats pass by.
